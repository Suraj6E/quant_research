Point-in-Time FX Research Database

A research database where every query can be answered as of a historical timestamp, returning only information that was actually available at that moment. The database is not the point — being able to detect and quantify look-ahead bias is.

Reading this dashboard. Every panel declares where its numbers came from. Live is measured from the repository right now. Recorded is a real measurement captured on {{ captured_at }}.
