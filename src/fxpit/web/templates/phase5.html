Cross-feed reconciliation

{{ m.card_open('Dukascopy vs HistData disagreement rate', 'demo', 'In basis points of bars compared. Success criterion #3 is that this number exists and is documented — not that it is small.', 'Phase 5') }} {{ m.figure(recon) }}

HistData is a second opinion, not a fallback. Where two independent feeds disagree materially on the same bar, at least one is wrong, and the rate is a data-quality finding in its own right. Anything that quietly reconciles them destroys a deliverable rather than merely hiding a bug.

Hypothesis H4 predicts disagreement clusters around rollover, news releases, and thin holiday sessions. High confidence it exists; the magnitude is genuinely unknown and is one of the more interesting numbers this project will produce.

The timezone anchor

{{ m.card_open('ECB daily fix as a drift detector', 'recorded', '') }}

The ECB reference rate is a single point per day — useless for trading, valuable as an anchor. If the reconstructed daily close diverges systematically from the ECB fix over months, there is a timezone or session-boundary bug. Silent timezone bugs are rated high-likelihood in the risk register, and this check is specifically designed to catch them.
